Tune 3MF

You open your Bambu Studio project, copy a prompt to the AI you prefer and paste its answer back. You get the same project with the settings tuned for that piece.

  • It only touches a closed list of settings, within safe ranges.
  • It shows every change with the reason; untick what you don't want.
  • It marks the changed settings as edited in Bambu Studio.

Why you can trust them

Every promise comes with a way to check it.

Your file never leaves your computer

Everything is calculated in the browser. Open the Network tab in your developer tools: there are no requests carrying your project. The only text that leaves is the prompt you copy, and you read it first. When you finish, you'll see one request to GoatCounter with just the tool's name: that's the counter.

Open source, MIT licence

You can read, copy and adapt it. See the code on GitHub.

Tested, and the AI's answer is never accepted blindly

Every change is validated against your file: unknown keys, out-of-range values and purge towers switched off in multicolour projects are rejected. See the automated tests.

Made in the Olipix Creative Lab workshop

Olipix Creative Lab is an independent studio: we design and print decorative pieces, and we like a challenge. The harder the fit or the shape, the more we want to try it.

These tools were born in our own workshop. We use them to prepare projects, quote and print our pieces, and we decided to share them: people who 3D print lose hours on the same problems, and a tool that already solves one of them shouldn't stay in a drawer.

We'll keep adding the ones that pass the test of daily use.
